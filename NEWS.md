## Lead stories

1. **World court: Myanmar committed genocide (The Hague).** The
   International Court of Justice rules 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide
   against the Rohingya in its 2016–2017 campaign in northern Rakhine State —
   the first time in the court's 80-year history it has held a state
   responsible for genocide. Myanmar is ordered to pay reparations, including
   facilitating the safe, voluntary and dignified return of refugees.
2. **Trump, Xi and Putin meet (Shenzhen).** The three leaders hold their
   first trilateral summit on the sidelines of APEC and issue a four-point
   peace declaration; resolving the Ukraine conflict is the US priority.
   Ukraine's European allies meet in Berlin and say they have a united
   position and are ready for talks with Russia.
3. **Anthropic soars, Wall Street sinks (New York).** Anthropic's record
   $81bn IPO — the largest ever — closes about 17% above its $83 price at
   roughly $97, after opening near $111.50. The wider market still falls:
   the S&P 500 loses 2.02%, its fifth straight drop; Japan's Nikkei drops
   4.68% and gold falls 2.42%.
4. **Houthi missile hits Riyadh tower (Saudi Arabia).** Yemen's Houthis
   strike Saudi Arabia's tallest tower in Riyadh and claim four targets,
   warning foreign investors to leave; Iran escalates tanker attacks in the
   Strait of Hormuz.
5. **Crimea Bridge hit again (Ukraine).** Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge
   a third time in four days; Putin vows a harsher response.
