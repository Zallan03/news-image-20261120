## 2. ICJ: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- The International Court of Justice ruled on 19 Nov (The Hague) that
  Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 crackdown —
  the first time in the court's 80-year history it has held a state
  responsible for genocide. Vote 9–6; ~10,000 killed; reparations ordered,
  Myanmar to report within two years.
- Sources: Le Monde, 20 Nov 2026 ("Rohingya: Historic ICJ ruling finds
  Myanmar guilty of genocide"); CNN, 19 Nov; NPR, 19 Nov; Amnesty
  International, 19 Nov; OHCHR, 19 Nov.

## 5. Voyager 1 — one light-day from Earth
- NASA confirmed on 19 Nov that Voyager 1 has passed one light-day from
  Earth — about 16 billion miles (26 billion km) — the first spacecraft
  ever to do so. Launched 1977; signal took about a day to reach Earth.
- Sources: Reuters, 19 Nov 2026 ("NASA confirms Voyager 1 probe's
  'light-day' distance milestone"); NASA/JPL statements.
